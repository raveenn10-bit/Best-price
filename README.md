# BestPrices.lk — Phase 01 Customer-Facing E-Commerce Storefront

**Developed by Harsh Apex Digital Solutions** (`https://harshapex.com.lk`)

## Overview
Complete, responsive Phase 01 customer-facing e-commerce frontend for **BestPrices.lk**, designed around Sri Lankan appliance and electronics retail benchmarks (`singersl.com`, `buyabans.com`, `daraz.lk`, `tondel.lk`) and the official **BestPrices.lk** red, black, and white brand identity.

## Included in This Repository
- **`public/standalone-preview.html`**: Self-contained zero-build interactive client demo with all 19 pages, 19 homepage sections, LKR formatting, live search autocomplete, category/brand/price filters, flash deal countdown, quick view modal, WhatsApp concierge, and `localStorage` persistence.
- **`src/data/storeData.ts` & `src/types/store.ts`**: Admin-Dashboard-ready isolated data layer (products, categories, hero banners, promo cards, sample reviews, FAQs, and business settings).
- **`src/app/`**: Next.js 15 App Router TypeScript structure covering all 19 required pages.
- **`public/assets/`**: Official `BestPrices-logo.png`, desktop & mobile promotional banners, and studio product photography.
